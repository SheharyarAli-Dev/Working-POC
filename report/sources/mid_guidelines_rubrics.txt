 FYP-1 Mid-Term Evaluation Guidelines & Rubrics
 Comprehensive Assessment Guide and Rubrics for Form 2 (Presentation & Demo.) and Form 3
 (Report Evaluation) of the FYP Handbook

 Executive Overview & Supervisory Evaluation Protocol
 The FYP-1 Mid Evaluation is a formative, supervisor-only assessment worth 20% of the overall FYP-1 grade.
 Conducted around Week 8 of the semester, its primary objective is developmental: acting as a diagnostic
 checkpoint where supervisors provide constructive coaching, verify implementation momentum, and
 ensure required proposal corrections are fully addressed prior to the summative FYP-1 Final panel defense.
 Key Evaluation Directives for Supervisors:

 • Form 2 (Presentation & Demo - 100% scaled to Form 2 score): Evaluates implementation progress,
 requirements precision, design evolution, repository hygiene, testing checks, and student ownership defense.

 • Form 3 (Report Evaluation - 100% scaled to Form 3 score): Evaluates the FYP-1 mid report across 8 core CLO-
 mapped criteria, checking report formatting, R&D depth, scope consistency, SRS requirements,
 architecture/design models, implementation reproducibility, and responsible tool disclosure.
 • Supervisory Action Gates: Enforces specific score caps and required action items (e.g., Panel Action Register
 Form 10, Individual Contribution Adjustment Form 11, or mandatory rewrites) whenever weak or missing
 evidence is detected.
