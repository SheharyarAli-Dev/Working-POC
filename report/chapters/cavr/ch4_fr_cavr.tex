FR-C9 & Verdict cache: store an analysis under a key of eight fields (ecosystem, name, version, artifact SHA-256, subtree digest, contract digest, policy version, analyzer version) and reuse it only when all eight match. & \path{test_hit_on_identical_inputs}; \path{test_miss_when_one_field_changes}; \path{test_only_verified_and_rejected_are_stored}; \path{test_tampered_row_is_ignored}.
