# Team review checklist

Please resolve these items from authoritative team, supervisor, or institutional records before submission:

- [ ] Obtain the proposal panel feedback/Form 10 and complete the action register with each action, response, owner, status, and evidence.
- [ ] Confirm the current FYP coordinator name and add required supervisory meeting records and approved decisions.
- [ ] Confirm the team schedule and member-by-member contribution ownership against task and review history, including the other module sections.
- [ ] Complete signatures, declarations, and remaining administrative details; verify names, IDs, dates, and required institutional fields.
- [ ] Independently verify related-work sources, bibliographic details, and the adequacy of the literature coverage.
- [ ] Have each member disclose their own external and GenAI tools, their use, and relevant data handling under institutional requirements.
